Export your Positions from Schwab as a CSV and save it in this folder.
Any filename works - it's auto-detected.

Options support is new and best-effort (built without a real Schwab
export to test against) - if your options don't show up right, email
back one example option row from your real CSV (you can blank out the
dollar amounts/quantities, just keep the Symbol/column format) and
it'll get fixed.
